## 1. Who it is for, and the one job of each screen

A general audience: anyone who wants to look back at their own recent activity, such as when a trip was and what they booked, without an account, a server or a cloud. The owner's photos and bookings are a test case, not the persona.

| Screen (iPhone tab) | One job |
|---|---|
| Timeline | Behind one unlock, show deterministic evidence for a chosen window, in four trails. |
| Ask | Answer a question over that evidence, streamed, with Stop and a tools-used row. |
| Add | Put one screenshot (a ticket, a confirmation) on the timeline, with sensitive text hidden before it is shown. |
| Privacy | Say plainly what iOS lets Pastport read, hold the Health toggle, hold Connected tools, and Lock and Clear. |

## 2. Screens

Existing structure (cited): `RootView` switches on `horizontalSizeClass` — tabs when compact, timeline plus Ask side by side when regular (`Sources/PastportiOS/PastportApp.swift:33-`); tabs are `TimelineModel.Tab.trail` and `.ask` only (`TimelineModel.swift:50-54`); scene-phase `.background` calls `lockAndClear()` (`PastportApp.swift:25-28`).

**Timeline (locked).** Before anything is read it shows: "Timeline is locked. Nothing has been read yet", the four window chips, an Unlock button, and one honest line: iOS does not let apps read Safari history. Data: none. Person can: pick a window, unlock. Today `start()` only sets a "closed" status (`TimelineModel.swift:155-167`) and `SessionLock` gates reads; the prototype's explicit locked screen is a presentation of that state, not a new gate. Owner of the type: `SessionLock` (`Sources/PastportKit/SessionLock.swift`) and `TimelineModel`.
- Error: Face ID mismatch shows "Could not unlock. Nothing was read." with Try again and passcode fallback (`LAContext` policy `deviceOwnerAuthentication`). New copy; behaviour exists in `Authenticating`.
- Empty (unlocked, nothing found): "Nothing to show yet. Allow read-only photo access, or add a screenshot of a booking." Existing status text: `PhotoEvents.statusLine(for:)` (`PhotoEvents.swift:28-`).
- Offline: banner "Offline is fine. Timeline, Ask and screenshots run on this iPhone. Only Connected tools need a connection."

**Timeline (unlocked).** Window chips, then the trail chips, then evidence cards. Trails on iPhone (new type `TimelineTrail`, four cases): Overview, Travel, Photos, Privacy. Note the Mac `HistoryFocus` has five (overview, research, travel, privacy, places; `HistoryJourney.swift:4-9`); the Mac enum is not reused, because research and places need Safari history. `TimelineTrail` is a separate small enum in `PastportKit`, not a subset hack.
- Each card leads with one human sentence, a mono source label (PHOTO ACTIVITY, SCREENSHOT YOU ADDED, HEALTH), and a closed "How do we know this?" disclosure: what was read, in which window, "Cleared when you lock or leave the app". Numbers on cards are formatted by code from `PhotoEvent` (`photoCount`, `screenshotCount`, `PhotoEvents.swift:19-25`), `AddedScreenshot` and (new) `HealthDay`. The model never produces a card number.
- Photos trail: `PhotoEvents.cluster` output (`PhotoEvents.swift:43-78` per the rehaul spec). Counts only; images are never opened.
- Travel trail: photo events whose scene labels or a locality cluster look like a trip, plus **booking cards from added screenshots** (section 2 "Add"). Deterministic rule: a screenshot becomes a booking card only when its scrubbed lines contain a date and a kind keyword (ticket, boarding, check-in, confirmation). New `BookingFromScreenshot` in `PastportKit`; the Mac `Bookings` detector works on Safari URLs and is unrelated (`Bookings.swift:1-40`).
- Privacy trail: "Nothing sensitive found" or a count of redactions by kind (`ScreenshotEvidence.redactionCount`, `ScreenshotReader.swift:35-45`). Never shows the redacted text.
- Overview: the newest card from each other trail, at most five.
- Duo/regular width: Ask sits beside the evidence (section 6).

**Ask.** Existing `AskScreen` and `TimelineAssistant` (`TimelineAssistant.swift:108-215`). Prototype adds nothing structural: chips for suggested questions per trail, streamed answer, Stop, tools-used list, note "Kept only because every number in it appears in the evidence. The model phrases; Swift counts." (existing policy `TimelineAnswerPolicy`, `:113-125`).
- Offline works (on-device). Model unavailable: banner "Apple Intelligence is off", answer replaced by the recorded facts (`TimelineEvidence.countsLine`, `:19-40`) with one line saying why (`FoundationTimelineAnswerer.reason(for:)`, `:198`). Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/fallback-apple-intelligence-not-ready-ios.png`.

**Add.** Photo picker (no library-wide permission needed) → `ScreenshotReader.read(imageData:)` (`ScreenshotReader.swift:94`) → scrubbed lines, kind, redaction count, then Add to timeline. The image is not kept. The prototype shows redaction as filled bars and the line "Emails, one-time codes and card numbers are replaced before you see it." Existing kinds are chat, banking, receipt, unknown (`:6-11`); this spec adds `ticket` and `lodging` only as classifier output used for the Travel trail (extend `ScreenshotKindClassifier`, `:162-`). Error: no text found → "No text found. Try a clearer screenshot." (new copy).

**Privacy.** Four cards: What iPhone can read (Photos activity counts, screenshots you add, optional Health totals; not Safari history), Health toggle (new), Connected tools (existing `ConnectedAssistant`, per-request Allow once or Decline, never receives timeline data, `ConnectedAssistant.swift:117-165` per the rehaul spec), and Lock and Clear.

## 3. On-device AI (Foundation Models)

Pattern reference: Apple's Origami sample — typed `@Generable` output, `LanguageModelSession.DynamicProfile` chosen by screen state (`Origami/Models/OrchestratorProfile.swift:13-64`), a tool that proposes and the person confirms (`Origami/Coach/MovePhotoToStepTool.swift`), `Error+DisplayMessage` for unavailability.

Today: one `LanguageModelSession` per question with three read-only tools `timeline_counts`, `photo_activity`, `added_screenshots` and `@Generable TimelineAnswerPayload { answer }` (`TimelineAssistant.swift:144-215, 238-281`). Unit tests use `TimelineAnswering` fakes and never call the model.

Change for 2.0:
1. **Profile per trail.** A `TimelineAssistantProfile: LanguageModelSession.DynamicProfile` keyed on the current `TimelineTrail`. Travel: tools `photo_activity`, `added_screenshots`, `bookings` (new, reads `BookingFromScreenshot`). Photos: `photo_activity`, `timeline_counts`. Privacy: `timeline_counts` and `redaction_summary` (new) only — it gets **no** screenshot text. Overview: the three existing tools. History transform keeps instructions plus the last four entries (same shape as Orbit `OrbitFoundationHistoryPolicy` and Breakline `CoachProfile.shortHistory`). Gated `#available(iOS 27, *)`; on iOS 26 the current `LanguageModelSession(tools:instructions:)` path stays (deployment target is 26.0, `project.yml:7-8`).
2. **Health tool.** `health_daily_totals` returns per-day steps and distance for the window from `HealthDay` values. Tool only registered when the Health toggle is on and authorization is granted.
3. **Typed output.** `TimelineAnswerPayload` gains `evidenceKinds: [EvidenceKind]` (`@Generable enum`: photoActivity, screenshot, health) so the UI can show which source labels the answer used. Code intersects it with the tools actually called; a claim with no matching call is dropped.
4. **Fallback.** Unchanged and required: when `SystemLanguageModel.default.availability != .available`, deterministic summary plus a reason line; no empty or invented answer. Unit tests for the Health tool and profile selection use fakes.

Model decides: which tool to call, the wording. Code decides: all counts, dates, windows, localities, which screenshot text is eligible, the number-subset check, and what Health data exists.

Observation from the sibling bench (not a Pastport result): the on-device model formats numbers loosely, so cards render numbers from data and the number check stays.

## 4. Data and safety boundaries

- Read-only everywhere: Photos activity (counts), screenshots the person adds, Health daily totals. Photos never download from iCloud (`PhotoLibraryProvider.swift:56-61`).
- Needs the person's approval: Face ID or passcode before a read (`SessionLock`); Photos permission at first read; Health permission only when the toggle is switched on; every Connected-tools call (Allow once).
- Everything read is cleared on lock and when the app leaves the foreground (`PastportApp.swift:25-28`). Health values and localities are in memory only. No account, no server, no analytics.
- No money handled; no `Decimal` in scope.
- Locality is derived per event and discarded with the event. Coarse by assumption A2. `PhotoRecord` carries no location today (`PhotoEvents.swift:5-15`); the slice adds a transient optional locality only inside the read pass.
- App Review 2.5.19: demo mode and `--ask` launch arguments are already inside `#if DEBUG` (`PastportApp.swift:14-22`) — the gate must re-verify after every slice. Entitlement note: `PastportiOS.entitlements:6-8` already declares HealthKit although no visible iOS Health feature exists today (only the Connected tool, `ConnectedAssistant.swift:103-112`). Slice 8 makes Health visible; if slice 8 is dropped, remove the entitlement and `NSHealthShareUsageDescription` from the iOS target in the same change.
- Run the `hidden-feature-gate` skill before reporting any slice done.

## 5. Accessibility

Reduce Motion: no pulsing or flashing anywhere (prototype uses none); streamed text appears in place and, under Reduce Motion, all at once. Dynamic Type: text styles only; evidence cards reflow, mono source labels wrap. VoiceOver: each card is one element reading "Source, sentence, N details"; disclosure announces expanded/collapsed; redaction bars read "Hidden: email address" (kind only, never content); Send/Stop have labels; locked screen reads "Timeline locked". Tap targets at least 44 pt.

## 6. iOS 27 and iPhone Duo

Layout from `horizontalSizeClass` and container width (`onGeometryChange`/`ViewThatFits`), never `UIScreen.main`, orientation or idiom; none exist in the iOS target today (confirmed by the rehaul spec section 6; re-grep in slice 1). Compact: four tabs (Timeline, Ask, Add, Privacy). Wide (Duo unfolded, iPad, large Pro Max landscape): the prototype's two panes — Timeline left with Ask beside it; Add beside Privacy — and the tab rail on the trailing edge. Pane choice is a pure function `PaneLayout.panes(for: Tab, width:)` in `PastportUI`, unit-testable. Folded Duo is the compact layout. Focus and keyboard order follow reading order. iOS 27 SwiftUI items to adopt where they remove code: `AsyncImage` is unused; swipe actions are unused; nothing here needs `reorderable`. Spotlight/App Intents: one `OpenTimelineIntent` (`supportedModes: .foreground`) that opens the **locked** screen and never returns data; no other intents in 2.0. Target stays `supportedDestinations: [iphone, ipad]`, `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY: "1,2"`, no Catalyst/compat flags (verify in `project.yml:81-84` already sets them; slice 1 pins them with a test).

## 7. Source matrix: what runs on iOS and what stays on the Mac (the QUEUE.md job)

| Source | iPhone/iPad 2.0 | Mac | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Safari history | No. Stated on screen | Yes (`SafariHistory.swift`, `/usr/bin/sqlite3`, Full Disk Access, not sandboxed) | iOS has no API; the app says so instead of faking it |
| Photos activity | Yes, counts and scene labels only | Yes | `PhotoLibraryProvider`, `PHPhotoLibrary` read-only |
| Added screenshots (OCR, scrub) | Yes, photo picker | Yes | `ScreenshotReader`, Vision on device |
| Bookings | From **added screenshots** only (new `BookingFromScreenshot`) | From Safari pages (`Bookings.swift`) and screenshots | Same user outcome, different evidence |
| Health daily totals | Yes, opt-in (slice 8) | No (HealthKit on Mac is thin) | `HKStatisticsCollectionQuery`, read-only |
| Contacts, Calendar, Reminders, Location, Music | Only as Connected tools with Allow once | Same | `ConnectedAssistant` |
| Ollama / localhost model | No | Yes | Mac-only code; no localhost client in the iOS binary |
| CLI, menu bar, Foundation Lab export | No | Yes | Mac surfaces |

## 8. Out of scope, and open questions for the owner

Out: Safari snapshot import (moves browsing history between devices; needs its own privacy design — if wanted, the safest shape is a Mac-exported, already-scrubbed summary file the person picks, never raw URLs), iCloud sync, widgets, Apple Watch, Android, any upload, signing, release.

Open for the owner (assumed answers above): Is Health worth the permission on first release (A1)? Is "near Ubud" the right place level (A2)? Do you want the iPhone to offer "All" as a window (A3)?
